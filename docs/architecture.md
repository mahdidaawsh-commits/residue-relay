# ResidueRelay: public coded-record reconstruction

A deployment fixes a cohort, five immutable fragment URLs/SHA-256 commitments, and an integer fault-weight budget. Anyone acquires one or more previously unseen positions in an atomic batch. Leader and validators independently fetch every full document, check position/cohort/bytes, interpret each final observed symbol and compare exact normalized residues. A separate audit checks source faithfulness, including explicitly unavailable symbols. No caller supplies authoritative observed values.

The public record is three coefficients of a degree-at-most-two polynomial over GF(17). Its five fragments are evaluations at x=1..5. This is public redundancy, not secret sharing or encryption. The budget is `2*errors + erasures`; the standard five/three code guarantees at most one compatible polynomial when the budget is at most two. Missing observations remain UNSEEN and block analysis/sealing; only acquired source-supported UNKNOWN values count as erasures.

After all five positions are acquired, `probe(weight)` analyzes a hypothetical budget and `seal()` freezes the configured decision. When at least three agreements are required, the contract interpolates every known triple, deduplicates polynomials and verifies the complete candidate list against all observed positions. If a permissive budget requires fewer than three agreements, it constructs two distinct compatible polynomials rather than claiming that triple interpolation enumerates all possibilities. A RECOVERED result requires uniqueness; AMBIGUOUS chooses no record; OUTSIDE_BUDGET means no compatible codeword exists under that budget.

Only a uniquely sealed record permits repair packets. Each packet binds its cohort, source commitment, position, observed value, corrected residue and polynomial commitment. Packets are recorded only for a differing or erased position, once per position. Original observations never change. Recording a packet does not claim that an external publisher applied it.

The contract owns source acquisition, normalization consensus, slot state, exact decoding/countermodels, immutable sealing and repair provenance. Clients own fragment publication, physical data collection and applying any proposed repair. The contract does not certify publisher honesty or that the true original record lies inside the chosen fault budget. Its conclusion is about codewords compatible with the acquired observations and explicit assumptions.

This is an algebraic reconstruction and repair primitive, not a graph proposal protocol, median oracle, service certificate or renamed concurrent-history auditor. The mathematical code is classical: [MIT 6.02 errors/erasures notes](https://web.mit.edu/6.02/www/f2009/handouts/lectures/L8.pdf) and [Stanford CS144 Reed–Solomon notes](https://www.scs.stanford.edu/10au-cs144/notes/l13-print.pdf). The contribution is this bounded GenLayer acquisition/reconstruction protocol with independently validated prose, ambiguity witnesses and repair provenance. Generic CLI/test bootstrap is reused under MIT.
